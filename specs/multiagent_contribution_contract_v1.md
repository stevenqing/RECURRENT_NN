## Contribution hierarchy

### Not a contribution by itself

- more agents with proportionally more memory;
- a sharded implementation of one global search trail;
- standard distributed CSP or conflict-directed backtracking;
- lower simulated latency obtained only by assuming more parallel hardware;
- an unconfirmed Qwen development result.

### Contribution A — Boundary law

Identify and validate the phase boundary where equal-total-resource factorization helps:

$$
\alpha=L_G/C>1,\qquad
\beta=\max_iL_i/C\le1,\qquad
\gamma=G(S,\rho)/\Delta F<1.
$$

The contribution is a falsifiable capacity–coupling law with holds-here/not-there regions, not “teams are better.”

### Contribution B — Mechanism

Show that dependency-local rollback preserves unrelated work and lowers **aggregate** search work relative to equal-memory global search. Centralized factorized and asynchronous independent controls must distinguish factorization benefit from decentralization overhead.

### Contribution C — Sparse frozen-LLM specialization

After Contribution B passes, show that a frozen LLM used only for cross-agent route selection beats random-valid and deterministic route controls under matched call/token/work budgets, while avoiding per-branch LLM calls.

## Required paper-grade evidence

1. Independent local trails, no global suffix rollback.
2. Equal-total-memory monolith and centralized-sharded controls.
3. Centralized-factorized control using identical local ordering.
4. Random-valid, deterministic, no-cross, and oracle route controls.
5. Aggregate work, critical path, mutable memory, LLM calls/tokens, and communication reported separately.
6. Development and confirmatory manifests generated independently.
7. At least one second substrate before making a general law claim.
8. Separate literature audit before using “novel” in title or abstract.

## Kill criteria

- No aggregate-work reduction: remove strong free-lunch claim.
- Route policies do not matter: remove LLM-routing contribution.
- Qwen does not beat random/deepest under matched budgets: retain symbolic factorization only.
- Effect disappears on a second substrate: scope the claim to planted separator graph coloring.

## Current evidence label

- Horizontal aggregate capacity: confirmed.
- Equal-resource expected-work advantage: independently observed in community SAT and graph coloring; graph also passes strict solve non-inferiority, while SAT retains a narrow conservative-bound failure.
- Independent factorization: confirmed as a capacity-valid route/work mechanism across two frozen symbolic substrates.
- Sparse Qwen routing: not authorized; deterministic matched-window routing leaves little quality headroom and a model call would add resource and validity risk.
